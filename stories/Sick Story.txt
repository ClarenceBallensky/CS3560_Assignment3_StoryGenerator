Title: Sick story
Reading Level: Teen


=== Chapter 1 ===
The world blurred beyond Maya's window. A persistent cough wracked her body, each breath a struggle. Days bled into nights, marked only by the chime of medicine and the gentle hand of her mom. Sickness held her captive, a heavy blanket. She yearned for sunshine, for laughter, for the simple freedom of a healthy day. Just one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== Chapter 2 ===
Sickness wasn’t just physical; it gnawed at her spirit. The world outside, once vibrant, now felt impossibly distant, a cruel reminder of what she was missing. Each tremor of a cough was a fresh defeat, another day stolen. She scrolled through old photos, healthy memories mocking her current fragility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== Chapter 3 ===
A new chill seized her, despite the persistent fever. Her throat felt raw, each swallow a painful rasp. The cough deepened, wrenching spasms leaving her breathless and weak. Her mom’s voice sounded distant, muffled by aches. This wasn't stagnant; it was actively, terribly worsening.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== Chapter 4 ===
The air grew impossibly heavy, a weight on her chest. Breathing became a shallow, desperate gasp. Her head throbbed relentlessly, sending waves of nausea. The room spun, lights blurring. A new, sharp pain etched itself into her ribs, making every cough a searing fire. This was truly terrifying.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== Chapter 5 ===
Her vision flickered, reality dissolving into a feverish haze. Distorted shadows danced on the walls, whispering unintelligible fears. Every inch of her body ached with a relentless, consuming fire. She couldn't tell day from night anymore, only the ceaseless, suffocating presence of the illness, tightening its grip.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== Chapter 6 ===
The fever grew hotter, making her skin burn. Her thoughts felt slow and fuzzy, like thinking through thick fog. Her tummy hurt, and she felt too weak to even lift her head. Everything felt heavy, pressing down. She just wanted the bad feelings to stop.
