Title: Plant story
Reading Level: Child


=== Chapter 1 ===
Lily loved playing in her backyard garden. One sunny morning, she heard a tiny rustle. A bright yellow daisy winked at her! "Good morning, Lily!" it chirped. Lily's eyes went wide. Then, the big oak tree whispered, "Hello!" its leaves gently swaying like waving hands.

A happy sunflower slowly turned its head to face her. Little rose bushes giggled, their petals wiggling with delight. All the plants in her garden were alive! They weren't just pretty; they were her new, green friends. Lily spent the whole day talking and laughing with her magical garden. It was the best day ever!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



=== Chapter 2 ===
The next morning, Lily couldn't wait to run outside. The daisy called, "Good morning, Lily-pad!" and the oak tree chuckled, its leaves rustling louder. "Sleep well, dear friend?" asked a wise old rose bush. Lily grinned. She told them about her favorite toy, a little red ball. The sunflower beamed. "A ball? Let's play!" it exclaimed, its big head nodding. Lily rolled the ball gently, and the plants cheered. The grass tickled her feet as she chased it, feeling like she was playing with a hundred new friends. Her garden wasn't just magic; it was the most fun playground ever!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



=== Chapter 3 ===
Lily giggled as the rose bushes caught her ball with their soft petals. "My turn!" chirped the daisy, sending a gentle breeze to push the ball back towards her. The big oak tree made a grand shadow. "This is even better than regular play," Lily thought happily. Suddenly, a little beetle scuttled across her path, heading straight for a juicy rose petal. "Oh no!" whispered the daisy. The wise old rose bush gently wiggled its stem, guiding the beetle away to another leaf. "We protect our friends," it said kindly. Lily felt a warm feeling. Her garden wasn't just fun; it was also full of caring, brave friends!
